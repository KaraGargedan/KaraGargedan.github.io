Privacy policy

Throne Room · last updated 21 October 2026

Who uses this app

Throne Room is a private tool used only by its owner to publish to and measure his own YouTube channels. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • YouTube upload and management (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): to upload the owner's videos, set their title, description and publish time, and post the owner's comment on them.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to read the connected channel's name and its videos' statistics.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's views and watch time.

How the data is stored

Login tokens and analytics are stored only on the owner's own computer, and tokens are encrypted. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Limited Use

Throne Room's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is revoked, the stored token stops working and the app can no longer reach the channel.
